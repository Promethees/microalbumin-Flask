Four modes, one dataset

Pick the mode, not the formula.

The same files carry through every mode. Switch from a raw trace to a fitted standard curve to a printable report without exporting anything in between.

kinetics

Time-series absorbance

Sliding-window regression across the whole trace, with the annotation lines drawn on the chart.

  • Max rate — highest ΔAbs/s
  • Slope over the full run
  • Saturation value
  • Time to saturation

point

Endpoint assays

Read a single time point across every source, with baseline subtraction when you need to compare channels directly.

  • Normalise to baseline
  • Multi-source splitting

One run, end to end

The path a measurement takes.

The desktop build drives the colorimeter itself, so a run goes from the cuvette to the finished report without leaving the app or re-typing a number.

Connect

Plug the colorimeter in over USB. The app reads its mode, active channels, live value and battery, and gives you a keypad to drive the menu without touching the device.

Record

Start a session with your own folder and filename. Readings land on the chart as they arrive — pause, resume, or take a manual point reading mid-run.

Analyse

Chart absorbance against time, zoom to a window, set the sliding-window size, and read the kinetics for each source.

Calibrate

Fit your standards with the regression that suits the chemistry, check R², and export the coefficients.

Derive

Load a saved curve and every measured value is converted to concentration next to its own analysis panel.

Report

Push snapshots into named subjects and publish the lot as HTML or an Excel workbook with charts.

Connect and Record need the desktop build — a browser tab cannot reach a USB device. From Analyse onwards the web app does the same work on files you already have.

What comes with it

Built around how a bench actually runs.

Your data, your disk

Everything lands in one folder — by default under your Documents, and movable to an external drive from App Settings. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Files handled in place

Create, rename, copy, move, merge and edit CSVs and their folders without leaving the app or opening a file manager.

Multi-source runs

Each measurement channel gets its own chart. Split by source, filter by source count, or normalise them all to baseline.

Five regression models

Linear, polynomial, logarithmic, exponential and Michaelis–Menten, with an R² threshold to drop poor windows.

Excel that keeps the maths

Exported workbooks carry the fitted curve as a live formula, so a value typed into the sheet returns a concentration — not a frozen number.

OKAPI Assistant

Ask about your data, your coefficients or the interface. It answers, or walks you through the app step by step. The one part that wants a connection.

The interface speaks English, Tiếng Việt, 中文 (简体), Français, 日本語 and Русский. Theme, default mode, time and concentration units and sort order are remembered between launches, and an update installs over the top without touching your data, reports or settings.
