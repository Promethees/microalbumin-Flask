    Who is responsible for your data

    The controller of the personal data described here is the Center for Bioscience and Biotechnology (CBBiotec), University of Science, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (HCMUS–VNU), 227 Nguyen Van Cu, Cho Quan ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Contact: tqmthong@gmail.com.

    This policy covers the website at www.easysensorkit.cbbiotec.vn, the hosted web application at /webapp, the Easy OKAPI desktop application, and the account, activation, licence-check, update and assistant endpoints behind them — together, the Service. It sits alongside our Terms of Service.

    Easy OKAPI is an academic research tool and is not intended to process personal health information. Please do not put patient identifiers into file names, source labels, report subjects or assistant prompts.

    What we never do

    • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data or your measurement data.
    • We do not use your measurement data, your files or your reports to train any machine-learning model, ours or anyone else's.
    • We do not build advertising or marketing profiles, and we send no marketing email. The only email we send is transactional: verification, password reset, and licensing notices.
    • We do not read your files for any purpose other than running the computation you requested — except where we must access a specific record to investigate a support request you raised or a security incident.
    • We do not store your password. We store only a bcrypt hash, which we cannot reverse.

    The desktop application

    The desktop build of Easy OKAPI is local-first. It runs a server on your own machine, reads the folders you point it at, and talks to the colorimeter over a USB serial port. Your measurement data is never uploaded to us as part of normal operation.

    What stays on your machine

    • Every CSV, calibration JSON, report and export you create.
    • An interaction event log under log/events/, written as one JSONL file per session. It records which actions were taken and when, so that a bug can be reconstructed. It is stored locally and is never transmitted automatically.
    • Your application settings and the activation file.

    What leaves your machine

    • Activation and licence checks. The application sends your licence token and the machine's hardware fingerprint to our server when you activate, and periodically thereafter so a revoked seat or banned account takes effect. Nothing else about the machine is sent.
    • The hardware fingerprint itself is a SHA-256 digest of a stable operating-system machine identifier (the Windows MachineGuid, the macOS IOPlatformUUID, or the Linux machine-id) combined with a fixed salt. We receive only the digest. It identifies a machine to us, but it is not reversible and it carries no name, serial number, MAC address, location or hardware inventory.
    • Update checks and downloads, which send the licence token so we can confirm your seat before serving a build.
    • Assistant messages, only when you use the assistant — see §06.
    • Bug reports, only when you send one. The application prepares a zip of the event log files you select and opens a draft email; you decide whether to attach it and whether to send.
    • Background music lookups, if you use that feature, resolve stream metadata over the network. No data about your work is included.

    Uninstalling the application removes it from your machine. To also remove the machine's seat from our records, delete your account or email us.

    The AI assistant

    1. When you use the assistant, your prompt, the recent conversation and a structured summary of the current screen are sent to Groq, which runs the language model and returns the reply. This applies both to the web application and to the desktop application when it uses our proxy.
    2. Anything you type into the assistant leaves your machine. Do not paste personal data, patient identifiers, credentials or confidential third-party information into it.
    3. We do not use your conversations to train a model. Groq's own handling of the request is governed by their terms and privacy policy.
    4. In the desktop application you may configure your own provider API key. Requests then go from your machine directly to that provider and never reach our servers.
    5. If you rate an assistant answer, the rating and the exchange it refers to may be stored so we can find and fix bad answers.

    Google Drive

    1. Connecting Google Drive is optional and off by default. The connection is made through Google's own OAuth screen, which shows you exactly what you are granting before you agree.
    2. The connection requests Drive access together with your basic profile and email address, so that files can be written to and read back from the folder you choose and so we can show you which account is connected.
    3. We use the authorisation only to push your Easy OKAPI session files to the folder you select, and to pull them back when you ask. We do not index, scan or copy the rest of your Drive.
    4. We store the OAuth tokens and the selected folder ID so the connection survives a page reload. Tokens are held encrypted at rest.
    5. You can disconnect at any time from the Drive panel, or revoke our access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already synchronised stay in your Drive, under your control — disconnecting does not delete them.
    6. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

    Cookies and local storage

    We use no advertising or analytics cookies. What the site sets is limited to what it needs to work:

    Name Kind Purpose and lifetime
    session Cookie — strictly necessary A signed cookie holding your session identifier and sign-in state. Set with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Lasts up to 30 days, and is also subject to an idle timeout.
    theme Local storage Remembers whether you chose the light or dark interface. Stays in your browser, never sent to us. Clearing site data removes it.

    The landing page opens no session storage at all: a visitor who never enters the web application creates no server-side record beyond an ordinary request log line.

    International transfers

    CBBiotec is in Vietnam, but the Service runs on infrastructure operated by providers based in the United States and hosted in their regions. Using the Service therefore involves transferring your data outside Vietnam and, if you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, outside those areas.

    We rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses and equivalent transfer mechanisms for those transfers, and we keep the data transferred to what the Service actually needs. If you would rather your measurement data did not leave your own machine at all, use the desktop application rather than the hosted web application (§05).

    Security

    • All traffic to the Service is over HTTPS.
    • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Plaintext passwords are never written to the database or to logs.
    • The session cookie is signed and set Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, so it cannot be read by page scripts or sent cross-site.
    • Permanent activation tokens are RS256-signed and verified against a public key held in the client, so a forged token fails verification offline.
    • Google credentials and OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest.
    • Account, download, activation and assistant endpoints are rate-limited to blunt credential stuffing, mail flooding and cost-drain attacks.
    • Access to production data is limited to the maintainers who need it to run the Service.

    No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires it. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to tqmthong@gmail.com before disclosing it publicly — we will work with you.

    Your rights, and how to use them

    Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to processing, to portability, and to withdraw a consent you gave. We apply these to every user, wherever you are.

    1. Do it yourself. Your name and email are editable from the account settings panel. You can delete your account there too, with password confirmation — that removes your account record, your social sign-in connections and your machine seats. You can disconnect Google Drive from the Drive panel.
    2. Ask us. Email tqmthong@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We will confirm your identity through the email address on the account and answer within 30 days.
    3. Exercising these rights is free. If a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive we may decline it, and we will tell you why.
    4. Deleting your account is irreversible and immediately disables every activated machine seat. Export the files you want to keep first.
    5. If you are unhappy with how we handled a request, tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, and in Vietnam to the competent authority for personal data protection.

    Children

    The Service is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16, or under the minimum age of digital consent where they live, whichever is higher. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to tqmthong@gmail.com and we will delete it.

    Where Easy OKAPI is used in undergraduate or secondary-school teaching, the supervising institution is responsible for obtaining any consent its own rules require.

    Changes to this policy

    We will update this policy when the Service changes. The version number and effective date at the top of this page always identify the current text. For a change that materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will notify you by email or in the application at least 14 days before it takes effect, unless a shorter period is required by law or by a security issue.
